# MBSA Lab

# Project Overview

---

## What is MBSA Lab?

MBSA Lab is an engineering platform dedicated to
Model-Based Safety Architecture.

Its objective is to bridge the gap between theory and industrial
practice by developing practical engineering demonstrations,
reusable patterns, educational resources and open-source tools.

---

## Vision

Become one of the leading open engineering references for
MBSA and practical systems engineering.

---

## Scope

The project covers:

- MBSE
- MBSA
- UML
- SysML
- Safety Engineering
- Eclipse Development
- Plugin Development
- Engineering Patterns
- Industrial POCs
- Architecture Frameworks

---
